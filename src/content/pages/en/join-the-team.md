## Who we are

We are a European BPO company where **Western standards meet an Eastern mindset** — multicultural and cosmopolitan at our core, skilled in technology, and experienced in both emerging and established markets. Our people understand the cultural context of the brands they represent.

## Sounds like you?

We encourage agile minds and self-driven people to join a friendly, open, conversational team. We are warm and eager to learn, and our team spirit is built on a healthy degree of autonomy. In return for job satisfaction, we look for a special kind of skills:

- ✓ speaking a language well — and talking nicely to people;
- ✓ explaining IT technicalities so that anyone understands them;
- ✓ ticking off requests and leaving peace of mind behind;
- ✓ enjoying a job of meaningful interaction, alongside people who want the same.
