## Outstaffing (staff leasing)

We supply specialists or teams for your project for the duration of a contract. You keep full control over their work; we handle recruitment, legal matters and payroll.

### How it works

<div class="grid-2">
<div>

### 1. Recruitment
Candidate sourcing, shortlisting, interviews and job offers.

</div>
<div>

### 2. Onboarding
Workspace and communication tools, product and culture introduction, performance tracking.

</div>
<div>

### 3. Management
Regular check-ins, progress tracking, problem resolution and contract compliance.

</div>
</div>

### Advantages

- **Team oversight** — manage leased staff as if they were in-house employees.
- **Cost savings** — lower overhead and employment costs than full-time hires.
- **Workforce agility** — end, extend or renew contracts easily, or build a new team after a project.
- **Focus** — you concentrate on delivery; we handle the organisational side.
- **Less risk** — fewer administrative tasks for HR and accounting, and reduced legal exposure.
- **Fast scaling** — add language skills or capacity for peaks without long hiring cycles.

## Frequently asked questions

<div class="faq">

<details>
<summary>What are staffing services and how can they benefit my business?</summary>

A staffing company sources, vets and places employees in your business, and can handle payroll and compliance. You get access to qualified candidates quickly, with less downtime and administration.

</details>

<details>
<summary>What types of roles can you provide?</summary>

From temporary and contract positions to permanent placements — customer support agents, technical support specialists, team leaders and other specialised roles, in 20+ languages.

</details>

<details>
<summary>How does outstaffing differ from traditional recruitment?</summary>

With outstaffing, the specialists are employed by us while working for you. We manage sourcing, interviews, onboarding and HR administration — your internal HR doesn’t have to.

</details>

<details>
<summary>How does staff leasing differ from temporary staffing?</summary>

Staff leasing is usually a longer-term arrangement that includes all HR-related tasks. Temporary staffing typically covers shorter periods and may not include full HR services.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Who is responsible for compliance and performance?</summary>

We are responsible for compliance with employment law and regulations; you oversee day-to-day work and output. Both are defined clearly in the agreement.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Is outsourced staffing more expensive than hiring in-house?</summary>

Service fees can look higher at first, but outsourced staffing usually saves money overall: fewer internal recruitment resources, lower risk of bad hires, and the ability to resize the team without long-term overhead.

</details>
