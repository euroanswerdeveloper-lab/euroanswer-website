We’re thrilled to announce a major strategic expansion: **Euroanswer** is heading into Central Asia! Starting this May, we’re significantly expanding our operations by launching **a new hub in** **Tashkent, Uzbekistan**. We’re doing this in close collaboration with a fantastic local partner.

This expansion is a huge milestone, putting Euroanswer right at the heart of a rapidly evolving digital world.

Our Founder and **CEO, Mr. Marian Raianu**, shares his bold vision:

_“This move into Uzbekistan is more than just growth; it’s a deep commitment to excellence and innovation that’s going to redefine what we can do globally. By bringing this dynamic new team together with our existing powerhouses in Romania, Macedonia, Ukraine, and Poland, we’re not just getting bigger—we’re forging a unified force. This means we can deliver truly bespoke, cutting-edge services and solutions that aren’t just tailored, but transformative for the complex demands of today’s digital world.”_

This exciting partnership in Uzbekistan will dramatically broaden what Euroanswer can offer, both in [services](https://www.euroanswer.co.uk/services/) and languages, unlocking incredible value for our diverse clients. Beyond boosting Euroanswer’s ambitious growth and reinforcing our dedication to top-notch service, Tashkent operation is set to **significantly enhance our operational reach and talent pool.**

Empowered by the motto **‘Deliver Globally. Talk Locally.**‘ as our guiding principle, we’re ready to redefine [service delivery](https://www.euroanswer.co.uk/customer-support/) standards worldwide, solidifying our position as a leader in innovative, client-focused **[solutions](https://www.euroanswer.co.uk/)**.
