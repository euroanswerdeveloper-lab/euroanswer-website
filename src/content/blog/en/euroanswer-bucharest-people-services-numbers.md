### **From the beginning**

The year is 2004. The team: 30 agents. The drive and enthusiasm? We dare say ever-growing.  

Today Euroanswer is an independent player in [BPO](https://euroanswer.co.uk/) with a steady conqueror mindset. Through multicultural [delivery centers](https://euroanswer.co.uk/contact/) across the region and with high expertise in „rare” East European languages, the company supports skills in more than [30 languages and 40 countries](https://euroanswer.co.uk/customer-support/).  

Bucharest represents the [company’s headquarters](https://euroanswer.co.uk/contact/), concentrating the largest number of agents of all the locations,  the remaining projects being radially split in the other cities (**Galati in Romania** and **Skopje in North Macedonia**) according to project delivery needs. The services offered in Bucharest cover a wide range of [industries](/#industries), including: **telecom, IT, banking, television, online design, printing services, energy, payments, food & drink.** 

If we’re talking about the general atmosphere that one can find at Euroanswer Bucharest, we will list the friendly, relaxed atmosphere that encourages an open dialogue between colleagues. Teams composed of members coming from different cultural backgrounds work together beautifully, as people like to spend time together at work and outside work too. In the views of our colleagues, following our latest survey, happiness at work is defined through the quality of the relationship between peers.  

Our team thrives in a spirit of self-determination and autonomy. We are warm, open, eager to learn and welcome colleagues. We cultivate inclusion and accommodate diversity. We are open learners and curious folks.  

### **In the words of our colleagues** 

What most people most appreciate here, at Euroanswer, are the possibility to gain experience in a professional environment, the schedule flexibility, and the opportunities to further develop skills in higher level positions.

### **And now in the words of our partners**
