In an effort to continuously create value for [our clients](/#industries), we are pleased to announce our expansion into Eastern Europe. Through a partnership with a local Ukrainian company, as of October, we will be offering [**our services**](https://euroanswer.co.uk/services/) from a new facility in Kiev.

Under the Euroanswer umbrella, and in collaboration with our Ukranian partner, new colleagues in the Ukraine will be joining forces with our existing teams in the [UK](https://euroanswer.co.uk/contact/), [Romania](https://euroanswer.co.uk/contact/), and [Macedonia](https://euroanswer.co.uk/contact/) to develop new competencies, drive and manage change, and provide solutions to keep pace with the growth and demands of the digital world.

Says Mr. [Marian Raianu](https://www.linkedin.com/in/marian-raianu-340398/), Global Business Development and Alliances Manager at Euroanswer, _“We are delighted to offer an expanded [**range of services and language**](https://euroanswer.co.uk/customer-experience/) skills to our clients through this Ukrainian partnership. Not only will the Kiev operation create valuable new employment opportunities in the Ukraine, it is also key to Euroanswer’s growth strategy.”_

Founded in 2004, Euroanswer is an independent player in [**BPO**](https://euroanswer.co.uk/) with a steady conqueror mindset. Through multicultural delivery centers across the region and with high expertise in „rare” East European languages, the company supports skills in more than [30 languages and 40 countries](https://euroanswer.co.uk/customer-support/).
